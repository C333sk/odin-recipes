Scone Recipe

Ingredients List

  • 3 cups all purpose flour, plus some extra for kneading
  • 1/2 cup of white sugar
  • 5 teaspoons of baking powder
  • 3/4 cup of butter
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup of milk

Directions

  1. Gather all of your ingredients
  2. Preheat your oven to 400f or 200c
  3. Combine your flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in to a large mixing bowl: cut in the butter with two knives or a pastry hammer and mix till it resembles fine coarse crumbs
  4. Whisk whisk your milk and the beaten egg in a small bowl, gradually stir in the flour mixture until it is moistened
  5. Turn out the combined dough onto a lightly flowered surface and knead the dough briely for 5 or 6 turns
  6. Pat or roll out your dough in to 1/2 inch thick round and then cut the round in to 8 wedgeshaped pieces and place on to your prepared backing tray/sheet
  7. Bake the scones in the preheated oven until they are golden brown on top, this should take roughly 15 minutes
